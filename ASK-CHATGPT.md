# Ask ChatGPT — free review and copy

Open Today → Ask ChatGPT, or Plan → Ask ChatGPT. This feature prepares text locally. It does not embed an AI model or call an API.

1. Choose the latest 1, 3, or 5 completed workouts. Only completed working sets are included; warmups and the unfinished session are excluded.
2. Optionally include your training preferences or recorded effort, form, and pain feedback. These are off by default. Training preferences include the goal, age group, available days, session duration, equipment categories, and reported restriction status.
3. Type your question and select Prepare preview. Review and edit every line. Changing selections clears the old preview; prepare it again.
4. Select Copy reviewed summary. Select Open ChatGPT, paste into a conversation, and send it yourself. You may use your existing ChatGPT account, including its free offering subject to current limits. No API billing or key is required.
5. Review suggestions before making changes yourself. ChatGPT does not edit your routines or records. The existing offline rule-based coach still works independently.

Photos, passphrases, body measurements, Health summaries, session titles, equipment identifiers/settings, and journal notes are excluded from generated summaries. Exercise names and your typed question can contain private information: inspect them before copying. Extremely large summaries are shortened with an explicit notice.

Nothing is sent automatically, and the ChatGPT link contains no workout data. Pasting and sending shares the text outside your encrypted device vault under your ChatGPT account's settings. There is no automatic import of replies. No claim is made about AI checking your technique or replacing in-person help.

The preview and question are temporary, are not saved in backups, and are cleared on closing the dialog or locking the app. Copying creates a readable clipboard copy outside app encryption. It may sync through your device's clipboard services and survive locking; replace the clipboard contents after use. Opening another app/tab can lock Iron Local, so copy before leaving. If automatic copying is unavailable, select text and use your device's Copy command. You can prepare a summary offline; ChatGPT requires an internet connection.

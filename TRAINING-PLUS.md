# Training improvements — free and on device

## More exercise guides

Plan → Exercise library now has 25 guides, up from 13. New movements include dumbbell bench press, dumbbell Romanian deadlift, dumbbell shoulder press, lateral raise, biceps curl, hammer curl, leg extension, dumbbell calf raise, incline push-up, bird dog, cable face pull, and bodyweight squat. Each has original start/movement diagrams, equipment setup, three technique cues, common mistakes, muscle areas, breathing, and logging conventions.

Diagrams are bundled offline and are simplified reference poses, not videos or form assessment. External demonstration/guidance links open only when selected and require internet. No copyrighted video was downloaded or embedded. A related variation is explicitly labeled. One-arm dumbbell row matches the supported single-arm row guide; ambiguous names such as Overhead press or Romanian deadlift are not guessed. Use specific library names for your actual movement. More library movements do not automatically add volume to the beginner program.

New reference examples: [ACE dumbbell chest press](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/19/chest-press/), [NASM dumbbell Romanian deadlift](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/dumbbell-romanian-deadlift), [NASM bird dog](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/bird-dog), [NASM face pull](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/face-pull). All other references are linked from each guide.

## Weekly preview

Today → Preview my week, or Plan → Week preview. The next seven local dates show strength templates, optional easy activity, recovery, or an already completed session. Strength cards show exercises, working-set counts, rep ranges, rest, equipment identifier, a provisional load or calibration prompt, and estimated duration. First exposures never invent starting weights.

This is a provisional snapshot of the saved training record. It does not fabricate completed future workouts or extrapolate stronger future loads. A new readiness check is required before each actual session. A pain/poor-recovery report today keeps previews paused until reassessed; logged movement pain also blocks progression. Actual finish times and the existing 48-hour recovery rule may move later slots, especially in a fixed early-morning window. Previewed weekdays are not clearance to train or a quota.

The time estimate assumes five minutes of general warmup, two minutes of setup per exercise, about 1.5 minutes of practice per exercise, roughly 0.8 minutes per working set, and configured rest between working sets, with ten minutes of extra allowance. It is a planning estimate, not measured performance or a guaranteed fit. Do less and stop at your time limit instead of rushing. Future previews do not consume an easier-session review, alter history, or start a workout.

## Muscle report

Progress → Muscle report, or Plan → Coaching → Muscle report. Select this local Monday–Sunday week or the previous week. The current week is partial through now. Completed positive-rep working sets count; warmups, unmarked sets, zero-rep sets, future sessions, and the unfinished workout do not.

A set contributes once to each tagged primary muscle group. Compound movements can count in multiple rows; adding the rows will exceed the unique working-set total. Supporting areas are reported separately. Lats and mid/upper back are grouped as Back; rear/front/side shoulder regions are grouped as Shoulders. Elbow-flexor work is grouped under Biceps. This is an approximate exercise classification, not measured muscle activation, exact stimulus, or a recommended weekly set quota.

Unknown custom exercises are listed with unclassified set counts instead of guessed muscle groups. A zero in a row does not prove a muscle was unused or justify adding volume. Exact movement names matter. Existing records are not silently renamed.

## Actual equipment increments

Plan → Coaching → Equipment steps, or an active exercise → My equipment & exercise notes. Save the smallest available increase and the same equipment identifier used in the workout. Confirm you checked that exact piece of equipment. Different identifiers keep their settings and histories separate.

The setting is the increase, not the starting resistance. Use ONE dumbbell's increase for paired dumbbell exercises; use your consistent total-added-load convention for plate-loaded machines. The setting is saved with its unit and converted for display if you change kg/lb. Converted values are approximate: still confirm what is physically available. Irregular stacks may not follow one constant increment; choose a real setting manually when necessary. An increment cannot model unknown machine ratios, minimum loads, or every plate combination.

New coached progression targets and calibration suggestions use a confirmed saved increment. The existing repeated-performance, technique, pain, and recovery checks still take precedence. Unconfigured exercises retain the existing optional default, clearly labeled unconfirmed in the weekly preview. Completed historical weights are never changed. Already active exercises retain their current step until you save their equipment form; custom routines continue to use their own increase field. Blank in the active equipment form removes that identifier's saved step.

## Verify a backup without restoring

Profile → Encryption & locking → Verify a backup without restoring. Select an encrypted JSON file and enter the passphrase used for that export. The app decrypts and validates it locally, then shows its scope and counts of sessions, routines, photos, body check-ins, and selected Health summaries. It does not write to the vault, merge photos, replace records, or download readable data.

Complete, workout-only, and photo-only backups are supported; encrypted reference workout summaries are not restorable backups and are rejected. Wrong passwords, tampered ciphertext, and invalid contents fail without changing current data. An old export still needs its old passphrase. Workout-only files exclude photos. Verification confirms that this file opens with its supplied password and has supported contents; it does not prove it is recent, complete relative to today's app, or factually accurate. Continue making complete backups regularly.

The verification dialog clears password, selected file, results, and pending display updates on close/lock. JavaScript/OS memory erasure cannot be guaranteed. No network request is made for verification. All new increment settings are inside the existing encrypted vault and normal encrypted backups. AI chat remains removed. No HealthKit, Apple Watch companion, paid service, or analytics were added.

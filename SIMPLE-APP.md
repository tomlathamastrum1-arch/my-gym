# The simpler app

The bottom menu has three screens: Today, Plan, and Progress.

1. Open Today and start your next workout.
2. Answer the four daily readiness questions when asked. Extra sleep, stress, and time fields are optional.
3. Log one exercise at a time: weight, reps, and done. Rest starts after a completed set. Open the exercise guide whenever needed.
4. Give the movement feedback when prompted, then finish your workout. Recommendations use your recorded performance and feedback; starting weights are not guessed.

Your profile icon opens Settings. Backup and lock controls are easy to find. Extra tools are inside a closed optional section: photos, body measurements, manual Health imports, detailed reports, custom routines, equipment settings, and reviews. Existing records are preserved.

You do not need photos, daily journaling, Health imports, detailed muscle reports, calendar tools, or multiple custom routines to begin. They can wait until they serve a clear purpose.

## Updating an existing installation

First export a full encrypted backup and keep its passphrase somewhere safe. Replace your hosted application files with the contents of this folder, keeping the same site address. The service worker installs the new version; close all app/site windows and reopen after the updated files are published. Do not clear website data or delete your vault to refresh the interface.

This folder contains application code, not your private workout records. Records stay encrypted on your device. Browser data can still be lost through clearing storage, device loss, or browser cleanup. Backups are essential, and a forgotten passphrase cannot be recovered by this app. A public hosting address does not make the app itself private; never upload your backups, profile exports, photos, or passphrase to the repository.

The app is a rule-based training journal, not a substitute for hands-on coaching or medical care. Exercise guides remain available on demand. The iPhone rest timer may not sound while the phone is asleep or another app is open.
